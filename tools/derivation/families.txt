# Word families: what a language builds from a base word instead of giving it an independent root.
# Written once here; tools/derivation/families.mjs creates the dictionary entries that do not exist yet (tools/lexicon/13-families.txt),
# tools/derivation/build.mjs writes the pairs to data/derivations.json. New entries inherit domain, era, culture and biome of their base.
#
#   base: type derived, type derived, ...          base = noun (or "v:bake", "a:dark", "adv:..." for another part of speech)
#   a + b => lemma                                  compound of two words (modifier + head); "=> lemma" is optional
# Types: agent act result quality abstract place diminutive feminine adjective privative adverb causative negative repetitive excess
# A derived word takes the part of speech its type implies (agent, place... noun; adjective, privative adjective; adverb; causative verb);
# write "a:", "n:", "v:" or "adv:" before the derived word to override ("a: stable" and "n: stable" are different words).
# Only write a family when the derived meaning is regular: "of X", "place of X", "young X", "female X", "one who does X", "without X".
