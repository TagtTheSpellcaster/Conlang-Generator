# Universal sentences: communicative needs that every human language has to cover.
# Sources: Wierzbicka's semantic primes (I, you, want, know, see, hear, good, bad, big, small, do, happen, live, die, here, now...)
# and the survival / social / orientation needs described in the comparative literature.
# Each line is a sentence frame: [a|b|c] slots are alternatives shown together, so one line teaches several words.
# Every combination of alternatives must be a sensible sentence.
@universal

## Greetings and courtesy
[Hello|Goodbye|Welcome]. | init=hello:p|goodbye:p|welcome:p
[Thank you|Please]. | init=thanks:p|please:p
I am sorry. | S=I | V=be:v | O=sorry:a
[Yes|No]. | init=yes:p|no:p

## Wishes and blessings
Good [luck|health|night]. | init=+good luck:n|health:n|night:n
Peace to you. | init=peace:n | A=to>YOU_O

## Needs and the body
I am [hungry|thirsty|tired]. | S=I | V=be:v | O=hungry:a|thirsty:a|tired:a
I am [sick|cold|hot|afraid]. | S=I | V=be:v | O=sick:a|cold:a|hot:a|afraid:a
My [hand|foot|head] hurts. | S=my hand:n|foot:n|head:n | V=hurt:v
I want [water|food]. | S=I | V=want:v | O=water:n|food:n
I want to [sleep|eat|drink]. | S=I | V=want:v | O=sleep:v|eat:v|drink:v

## Danger and emergency
[Danger|Fire]! | init=danger:n|fire:n
[Run|Hide|Stop]! | mood=imperative | seq=V | V=run:v|hide:v|stop:v
Help me! | mood=imperative | seq=V,O | V=help:v | O=ME
Do not [touch|eat|take] this. | mood=imperative | seq=V,O | V=touch:v|eat:v|take:v/neg | O=THIS

## Affection and kinship
I [love|trust|help] you. | S=I | V=love:v|trust:v|help:v | O=YOU_O
You are my [friend|mother|father|child]. | S=YOU | V=be:v | O=my friend:n|mother:n|father:n|child:n
You are not my [enemy|friend]. | S=YOU | V=be:v/neg | O=my enemy:n|friend:n
My [mother|father|child] [loves|helps] me. | S=my mother:n|father:n|child:n | V=love:v|help:v | O=ME

## Identity
Who are you? | seq=init,V,S | init=WHO | V=be:v | S=YOU
What is your name? | seq=init,V,S | init=WHAT | V=be:v | S=your name:n
[Who|What] is this? | seq=init,V,S | init=WHO|WHAT | V=be:v | S=THIS
I come from [here|there]. | S=I | V=come:v | A=from>here:adv|there:adv

## Place and resources
Where is the [water|food|road]? | seq=init,V,S | init=WHERE | V=be:v | S=the water:n|food:n|road:n
Where is my [mother|child|friend]? | seq=init,V,S | init=WHERE | V=be:v | S=my mother:n|child:n|friend:n
The [water|food] is [here|there]. | S=the water:n|food:n | V=be:v | O=here:adv|there:adv
[Come|Stay] here. | mood=imperative | seq=V,O | V=come:v|stay:v | O=here:adv
[Where|When|Why] do you go? | seq=init,S,V | init=WHERE|WHY|when:c | S=YOU | V=go:v

## Knowledge and perception
I [hear|see|smell] you. | S=I | V=hear:v|see:v|smell:v | O=YOU_O
I do not [know|understand]. | S=I | V=know:v|understand:v/neg
[Look|Listen|Wait]! | mood=imperative | seq=V | V=look:v|listen:v|wait:v

## Quality and quantity
[This|Everything] is [good|bad]. | S=THIS|everything:pr | V=be:v | O=good:a|bad:a
This is [large|small|long|short]. | S=THIS | V=be:v | O=large:a|small:a|long:a|short:a
This is [much|little|enough]. | S=THIS | V=be:v | O=much:d|little:d|enough:d

## Time and events
Today we [work|eat|sleep]. | seq=init,S,V | init=today:n | S=WE | V=work:v|eat:v|sleep:v
Tomorrow we [travel|go|come]. | tense=future | seq=init,S,V | init=tomorrow:n | S=WE | V=travel:v|go:v|come:v
Yesterday I [came|went|ate]. | tense=past | seq=init,S,V | init=yesterday:n | S=I | V=come:v|go:v|eat:v
The [sun|moon] rises. | S=the sun:n|moon:n | V=rise:v
The [child|bird|dog] [lives|dies]. | S=the child:n|bird:n|dog:n | V=live:v|die:v

## Basic sentence patterns
I eat [food|bread]. | S=I | V=eat:v | O=food:n|bread:n
I [give|bring] you [water|food]. | S=I | V=give:v|bring:v | O=water:n|food:n | A=to>YOU_O
Give me [water|food]. | mood=imperative | seq=V,O,A | V=give:v | O=water:n|food:n | A=to>ME
The [good|small|old] [dog|child] [sees|hears] the [small|large] [bird|fish]. | S=the +good|small|old dog:n|child:n | V=see:v|hear:v | O=the +small|large bird:n|fish:n
The [dogs|birds] see the [children|fish]. | req=plural | S=the pl dog:n|bird:n | V=see:v | O=the pl child:n|fish:n
We [see|hear] two [dogs|birds]. | req=dual | S=WE | V=see:v|hear:v | O=du dog:n|bird:n
You cannot [enter|leave]. | S=YOU | V=enter:v|leave:v/neg,can
I can [help|follow|find] you. | S=I | V=help:v|follow:v|find:v/can | O=YOU_O

## Yes/no questions
Do you understand? | q=yn | S=YOU | V=understand:v
Are you [hungry|thirsty|tired|afraid]? | q=yn | S=YOU | V=be:v | O=hungry:a|thirsty:a|tired:a|afraid:a
Is the [water|food|road] here? | q=yn | S=the water:n|food:n|road:n | V=be:v | O=here:adv
Can you help me? | q=yn | S=YOU | V=help:v/can | O=ME

## Blessings and wishes (optative)
May you live. | mood=optative | S=YOU | V=live:v
May the [gods|spirits] protect you. | mood=optative | S=the god:n|spirit:n pl | V=protect:v | O=YOU_O | req=plural

## Reasons and conditions
I know that [he|she] is [here|good]. | S=I | V=know:v | comp=that:c ~ S=HE|SHE ~ V=be:v ~ O=here:adv|good:a
I come because I am [hungry|thirsty|afraid]. | S=I | V=come:v | adv=because:c ~ S=I ~ V=be:v ~ O=hungry:a|thirsty:a|afraid:a
If you come, I help you. | adv=if:c ~ S=YOU ~ V=come:v | S=I | V=help:v | O=YOU_O
I [eat|sleep] when I am tired. | S=I | V=eat:v|sleep:v | adv=when:c ~ S=I ~ V=be:v ~ O=tired:a

## Comparison
The road is [longer|larger|smaller|shorter] than the river. | S=the road:n | V=be:v | O=cmp long:a|large:a|small:a|short:a | A=than:c>the river:n
This is [better|worse] than that. | S=THIS | V=be:v | O=cmp good:a|bad:a | A=than:c>THAT

## Ongoing and completed actions
I am [eating|drinking|sleeping]. | S=I | V=eat:v|drink:v|sleep:v/prog
I have [arrived|eaten|slept]. | S=I | V=arrive:v|eat:v|sleep:v/perf
