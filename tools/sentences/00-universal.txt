# Universal sentences: communicative needs that every human language has to cover.
# Sources: Wierzbicka's semantic primes (I, you, want, know, see, hear, good, bad, big, small, do, happen, live, die, here, now...)
# and the survival / social / orientation needs described in the comparative literature.
@universal

## Greetings and courtesy
Hello. | init=hello:p
Goodbye. | init=goodbye:p
Thank you. | init=thanks:p
I am sorry. | S=I | V=be:v | O=sorry:a
[Yes|No]. | init=yes:p|no:p

## Wishes and blessings
Good luck. | init=+good luck:n
Good health. | init=+good health:n
Peace to you. | init=peace:n | A=to>YOU_O

## Needs and the body
I am hungry. | S=I | V=be:v | O=hungry:a
I am thirsty. | S=I | V=be:v | O=thirsty:a
I am [cold|hot]. | S=I | V=be:v | O=cold:a|hot:a
I am sick. | S=I | V=be:v | O=sick:a
My hand hurts. | S=my hand:n | V=hurt:v
I want [water|food]. | S=I | V=want:v | O=water:n|food:n
I want to sleep. | S=I | V=want:v | O=sleep:v

## Danger and emergency
Danger! | init=danger:n
Run! | mood=imperative | seq=V | V=run:v
Help me! | mood=imperative | seq=V,O | V=help:v | O=ME
Do not touch this. | mood=imperative | seq=V,O | V=touch:v/neg | O=THIS

## Affection and kinship
I love you. | S=I | V=love:v | O=YOU_O
You are my [friend|mother|child]. | S=YOU | V=be:v | O=my friend:n|mother:n|child:n
You are not my enemy. | S=YOU | V=be:v/neg | O=my enemy:n
My [mother|child] loves me. | S=my mother:n|child:n | V=love:v | O=ME

## Identity
Who are you? | seq=init,V,S | init=WHO | V=be:v | S=YOU
What is your name? | seq=init,V,S | init=WHAT | V=be:v | S=your name:n
I come from [here|there]. | S=I | V=come:v | A=from>here:adv|there:adv

## Place and resources
Where is the water? | seq=init,V,S | init=WHERE | V=be:v | S=the water:n
Where is the food? | seq=init,V,S | init=WHERE | V=be:v | S=the food:n
What is this? | seq=init,V,S | init=WHAT | V=be:v | S=THIS
The [water|food] is [here|there]. | S=the water:n|food:n | V=be:v | O=here:adv|there:adv
Come here. | mood=imperative | seq=V,O | V=come:v | O=here:adv

## Knowledge and perception
I do not know. | S=I | V=know:v/neg
I do not understand. | S=I | V=understand:v/neg
I hear you. | S=I | V=hear:v | O=YOU_O

## Quality and quantity
This is [good|bad]. | S=THIS | V=be:v | O=good:a|bad:a
This is [large|small]. | S=THIS | V=be:v | O=large:a|small:a
This is [much|little]. | S=THIS | V=be:v | O=much:d|little:d
Everything is good. | S=everything:pr | V=be:v | O=good:a

## Time and events
Today we work. | seq=init,S,V | init=today:n | S=WE | V=work:v
Tomorrow we travel. | tense=future | seq=init,S,V | init=tomorrow:n | S=WE | V=travel:v
Yesterday I came. | tense=past | seq=init,S,V | init=yesterday:n | S=I | V=come:v
The sun rises. | S=the sun:n | V=rise:v
I live. | S=I | V=live:v

## Basic sentence patterns
I eat [food|bread]. | S=I | V=eat:v | O=food:n|bread:n
I give you [water|food]. | S=I | V=give:v | O=water:n|food:n | A=to>YOU_O
The good dog sees the small bird. | S=the +good dog:n | V=see:v | O=the +small bird:n
The dogs see the children. | req=plural | S=the pl dog:n | V=see:v | O=the pl child:n
We see two dogs. | req=dual | S=WE | V=see:v | O=du dog:n
You cannot enter. | S=YOU | V=enter:v/neg,can
I can help you. | S=I | V=help:v/can | O=YOU_O
